## 1. What this phase reproduces

Roadmap P12 opens a new work stream: connectivity between channels, stimulus versus no stimulus, one averaged diagram per condition. "Coherence chosen first. The first real run (S03, S04) exposed two methodological bugs" [1]. The phase reproduces both bugs first, then fixes them: (a) PF-15, per-window coherence averaged over windows is biased upward, so the concatenated estimator is needed [4, 1]; (b) PF-16, a broadband baseline compared with a ±1 Hz stimulus band is not apples to apples, so the same 201 windows are re-scored in each stimulus band [4, 1]. The acceptance, verbatim from the roadmap [1]: "synthetic tests of task 2; S03/S04 baseline coherence drops after the fix (R-11: from ≈0.68–0.73 to ≈0.59–0.66); differences show 24 Hz and 20 Hz consistently positive in S03/S04 (≈+0.11 to +0.25) and 10.91/8.57 Hz inconsistent (alpha-band ceiling hypothesis recorded, to be tested statistically in P13)."

The acceptance windows A0–A6 were fixed in the pre-run audit, before any real-data run, and the results review could not move them [7]. The "baseline coherence" of R-11 is defined by the amended settled point 2 (§9) [8, 9].

## 7. Interpretation

**Acceptance A0–A5, as pre-committed.** A0 exact (197 and 197 stimulus windows); A1 PASS on the synthetic demo; A2 PASS for S03 and S04 (all four values inside their windows, both drops present); A3 PASS (10 of 10); A4 PASS (4 of 4, robust under the aligned diagnostic); A5 consistent with R-11 in all four values [6, 7].

**Alpha-band ceiling hypothesis (recorded, to be tested in P13).** The 10.91 and 8.57 Hz differences are near zero or negative in S03 (−0.0366, +0.0414) and clearly positive in S04 (+0.1934, +0.1286) [7].
- *Ceiling hypothesis (roadmap).* At 8–12 Hz the no-stimulus fixation already carries strong occipital alpha, so the matched baseline is high and a stimulus has little room to raise coherence. Supporting: S03's matched baseline at 104 and 105 (0.6435, 0.7390) is higher than S04's (0.5831, 0.6422), and S03 is the subject with no gain. Against a pure ceiling: S03 105's matched baseline (0.7390) is far below 1, and in S03 104 the stimulus is *below* its baseline (0.6069 raw against 0.6435 matched); a ceiling predicts no gain, not a loss [7].
- *Competing explanation (recorded, not tested).* Visual stimulation suppresses occipital alpha (alpha desynchronisation), which lowers alpha-band coherence under stimulation and can cancel an SSVEP gain in subjects with strong resting alpha. The S03 104 sign (stimulus below baseline) is consistent with this reading and not with a pure ceiling [7].
- *Third candidate.* Junction leakage. The A6 diagnostic does not support it here (S03 104 Δ −0.0133; S03 105 Δ +0.0565, the wrong sign for junction bias) [7].
- **P13 must test** (a) the per-band trial-level permutation significance of 104 and 105 for each subject, and (b) whether the matched-baseline level predicts the difference across subjects and bands. Two subjects cannot decide this; the five-subject P13 cohort is the minimum [7, 9].

**Junction sensitivity (F2) and A6 wording.** Concatenation without tapering splices Welch segments across window boundaries, and the boundaries are true discontinuities only between trials. The reviewer counted 7 of 79 segments (8.9 %) affected for S03 101 and about 38 of 155 (24.5 %) for the 201 baseline (`.venv/bin/python -c "print(7/79, 38/155)"` → `0.0886 0.2452`) [7, 9]. The reviewer's synthetic simulation predicted a downward bias at low frequency. On S03 and S04 the aligned diagnostic does not show it: the signs of Δ are mixed and eight of ten |Δ| are below 0.03. Band 105 of S03 is therefore described as **estimator-sensitive**, never as junction-biased, because its sign is opposite to the predicted direction [7, 9]. The junction concern is not material for S03 and S04 at P12, and P13 keeps the aligned diagnostic and re-checks it when permuted windows are re-concatenated [9].

**Colour scales.** The difference panels use symmetric limits per subject (§4.8). Any cross-subject figure in P13 must use one shared limit or state that it does not [9, 10].

**Per subject, data source and scope.** Every value is S03 or S04 only, legacy 7-channel folders, descriptive. The results do not generalise to the cohort (R9) [1, 7].
