No max-norm and no extra constraint for the two new networks (not in the roadmap) [7].

## 7. Interpretation

- **Conclusive.** Both networks build with R-8's counts exactly (2,349 / 14,485 / 56,021) and both benchmarks run on CPU (6 of 6 commands exit 0 in §3 counting both runs of each benchmark and the count script). The S03 gate (197 / 3, 7 channels) equals the classical flagship's and P08's. Each network repeats itself over two runs on this machine (§4.3).
- **Ordering on S03 (the only accepted statement).** Under the legacy leaky protocol on CPU, the order is ShallowConvNet > EEGNet > Compact-CNN, at both 1.0 s and 2.0 s: 171 > 149 > 107 and 183 > 169 > 124 (windows of 197; EEGNet from P08 CPU) (`decisions.md` P09 row) [5]. This is one subject, one device and one seed set, with leaky CV. It is not a cohort ranking and not a best-network claim. The differences exceed the few-window band (P08 device gap, 9 windows; binomial SE at 1.0 s is 3.55 pp for Compact-CNN and 2.41 pp for ShallowConvNet, `python3 -c "import math; print(round(100*math.sqrt(0.5431*0.4569/197),2), round(100*math.sqrt(0.868*0.132/197),2))"` → `3.55 2.41`). That does not make the S03 ordering a cohort result.
- **ShallowConvNet against R-9b and R-7.** Within a few windows of R-9b OFF (+2 / +3) and of R-7 (−5 at 1.0 s). No claim about augmentation or leak effect is made from these.
- **Compact-CNN against R-9b.** Compact-CNN is 23 / 32 windows above its leak-free OFF value. That is the direction PF-01 predicts, but with K = 29 under the fs 250 label, a different implementation and one subject, the attribution is not supported: inconclusive. P11 measures the leak with grouped CV.
- **PF-30 reading (train versus test).** Final training accuracy is 100.00 % in all ten folds for both networks (eval mode, un-augmented training fold) [5, 9]. Test accuracy: compact 48.72–58.97 % per fold (mean 54.31 %); shallow 79.49–92.31 % (mean 86.80 %); chance is 20 % for five classes. Following PF-30 [4]: high training accuracy with test well above chance is a generalization problem, not an optimization problem. The gap is 45.69 pp (compact) and 13.20 pp (shallow) (`python3 -c "print(round(100-54.31,2), round(100-86.80,2))"` → `45.69 13.2`). The gap is larger for Compact-CNN, whose head holds 73.12 % of its parameters on 32 × 256 = 8,192 unpooled features (§2). Over-parameterisation of the head is a plausible reading of the larger gap; it is **not tested** here (no ablation). This is a reading for P10–P11, not a result.
- **Smoothing.** The 2.0 s gains (+17 compact, +12 shallow windows, `python3 -c "print(124-107, 183-171)"` → `17 12`) are legacy `i % 5` figures on the clean array (PF-02) [4]. They are not a measured 2 s decision accuracy.
- **Inputs.** fs 250 is a label for ShallowConvNet (unused). For Compact-CNN it sets K = 29 (PF-04). Input is the z-scored legacy 7-channel window.
