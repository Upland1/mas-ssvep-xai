## 1. What this phase reproduces

Two sanity-check scripts of the original project, `diagnose_occipital_spectra.py` and `visualize_data.py` (roadmap P03, "Origin") [1]: before any model is trained, show per subject and per class whether the stimulus windows carry spectral peaks at the stimulation frequencies. The question: which of S01–S05 have visible fundamental SSVEP peaks in the occipital channels, and what is seen for the subjects without them. No classifier and no accuracy are involved. The acceptance is the roadmap line "figures exist; the Reviewer confirms visible fundamental peaks for the strong responders and records what is seen for S01/S02" [1].

## 6. Before / after

The one defect found by the review was in the reporting, not in the data. Round 1 audit (FAIL, `b4bc757`): the printed target-bin power read the bin nearest h·f0 on the relabelled fs-250 axis, which is not the bin a true response occupies for 24 Hz (nearest-labelled bin 25, response bin 24), 2×15 Hz (31 vs 30) and 2×8.57 Hz (18 vs 17) (`07_review.md`) [6]. At the default fs the log would have shown "24 Hz ≈ ×1 median" for S03–S05 whose 24 Hz response sits in bin 24: S03 101 ×0.52 (nearest-labelled bin 25) vs ×6.09 (response bin 24), S04 101 ×0.84 vs ×9.63, S05 101 ×0.96 vs ×9.12 (`diagnose_occipital_spectra_fs250_20261024T180123.log`). The fix (`1869c0a`) adds the response bin `round(h·f0·T/header_fs)`, keeps the nearest-labelled bin as the legacy reading (printed only when it differs), and adds the specificity ratio. Round 2 audit PASS; the run used the fixed code. The figures, default fs and axis were not changed.

## 7. Interpretation

- Conclusive: S03 and S04 show clear class-specific fundamentals for all five targets; S05 for all five, of which 104 and 105 are carried by the second harmonic (band maximum at 22 and 17 Hz). Power at the stimulation frequency is localised to the right class (specificity ×2.17 to ×20.92 in S03–S05, fs 256 log).
- S03 8.57 Hz: partial in P02's 7-channel mean (plateau at bins 8–9), clear in the occipital O1/Oz/O2 mean (bin 9, ×4.63, specificity ×2.43). The P02 partial is attributed to the channel set, not to the data (reviewer) [6].
- S01: no clear response at 24 or 20 Hz (×0.88, ×1.41); 101 peaks at 10 Hz as in P02 (band maximum 10 Hz, bin 10, P = 1.556e+05). Class 104 passes the rule but is alpha-adjacent. Band maxima lie at 8–11 Hz in all classes.
- S02: no class is clear or visible; the band maximum is at 6 Hz in every class, with a 9 Hz peak in every class, so classes 104/105 are alpha-confounded.
- Consistent with, but not a test of, the R-6 levels (S01 61.00 %, S02 56.12 % at 1.0 s; S03–S05 86.80 %, 97.46 %, 95.00 %; `mas/RULES.md`): no accuracy is computed in P03. Expectation recorded for P04/P05: S01/S02 near chance-to-moderate, S05 104/105 gaining from harmonics; an S01/S02 accuracy far above R-6 is a leakage suspect.
- Not conclusive: whether S01 104 is a genuine 10.91 Hz response or alpha; the origin of the S01/S02 low-frequency (6 Hz, 8–11 Hz) dominance (alpha, drowsiness or electrode contact cannot be separated here). Descriptive means over windows and the three channels, no uncertainty and no p-values.
