## 1. What this phase reproduces

Roadmap P11 derives from the project's critical finding: "every benchmark used `StratifiedKFold` on 1 s sub-windows, so the 5 sub-windows of one 5 s trial landed in both train and test. Fixed with `build_trial_ids` + `StratifiedGroupKFold` in all 9 benchmark scripts" [1]. The phase answers four questions under the leak-free protocol: (i) does the flagship (R-6) come back exactly; (ii) how large is the leak for the classical pipeline and for the networks; (iii) do the three augmentation A/Bs fall in the R-9b bands; (iv) does the before/after table show the drops and gains the roadmap predicts? Acceptance, verbatim from the roadmap [1]: "flagship = **R-6** … augmentation A/B = **R-9b**; the before/after table shows the classical pipeline dropping ~4–5 pp and the CNNs dropping much more (Compact-CNN up to ~20 pp on single subjects); the EEGNet cohort augmentation gain shrinks from +7.34 pp (last leaky run) to +2.01 pp, while Compact-CNN (+17.16 pp) and ShallowConvNet (+2.53 pp) keep theirs."
