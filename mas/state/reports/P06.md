## 7. Interpretation

**Conclusive, within the legacy protocol.**
- Window counts (197 clean, 3 rejected) hold on every row and match R-5 [17, 18].
- Four of five R-4 rows reproduce exactly, and all ten R-5 values match P05 [17, 18, 6].
- The Full row equals the P05 flagship on S03 [13].

**Not conclusive.**
- **The SFS row.** SFS and the full baseline differ by one window at each accuracy (0.51 pp). That is a tie, not a gain (§9, D-1). The SFS row moves between 172 and 177 windows at 1.0 s across the inner-CV and estimator variants with 15 columns, and reaches 166 with the 55-column candidate set [19]. Its fold-to-fold column sets differ, and only column 9 appears in all five [19]. This instability means the SFS position is not a finding.
- **Reductions.** Every reduced set is at or below the full baseline at 1.0 s, by 0.51–4.07 pp (one to eight windows): pruning 3.05 pp (6.01 windows), ANOVA 4.07 pp (8.02), MI 2.54 pp (5.0), SFS 0.51 pp (1.0). Arithmetic: `.venv/bin/python -I -c "…"` with `ref=90.36` gives those values [17]. With one subject, a leaky split and no interval, no reduction is singled out and the ordering is not interpretable.
- **The 2.0 s gains** (+11.0, +5.61, +5.07, −0.5, 0.0 pp) are partly a label-order effect of the legacy arrays under `i % 5` (P05 §7) [7, 18]. They are not decision gains. P11 measures the honest value.
- **The leak size** is not measured. Every P06 number is leaky (R1); P11 measures it with grouped CV on the same features [2, 14].

**Per subject (R9, data source: legacy folders `data/processed/<S>/`, OO session).** S01 (64.5 / 75.5) and S02 (66.33 / 71.94) stay low. S03 (90.36 / 95.43) is high. S04 (98.98 / 98.48) and S05 (96.5 / 96.5) are near ceiling. The spread is large and the cohort mean (83.33 / 87.57) hides it [18]. The ablation covers S03 only, as the roadmap prescribes [1].
