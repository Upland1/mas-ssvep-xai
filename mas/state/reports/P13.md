## 1. What this phase reproduces

Roadmap P13 origin: "PLV/wPLI, PDC and a permutation + Benjamini-Hochberg framework were added; a correctness review found two implementation bugs; the cohort script had a display bug; permutations were later moved from window level to trial level" [1]. The phase reproduces the bugs first and then fixes them: PF-17 (strided versus contiguous MVAR coefficient slicing), PF-18 (filtfilt + Hilbert edges versus CSD phase), PF-19 (window-level versus trial-level permutations), PF-20 (criterion A, FDR edges, and criterion B, global p, reported separately), and PF-03 (approximate trial ids) [4, 1]. The acceptance, verbatim from the roadmap [1]: "synthetic tests (MVAR recovery, edge immunity, trial split sizes); cohort result = **R-12** (S01/S02 null on essentially everything; 24 Hz the only frequency robust across S03–S05 in both coherence and wPLI; FDR edge totals per subject coherence 0/0/65/51/15, wPLI 0/3/38/33/18 at 200 trial-level permutations within tolerance); borderline cells that flip between 100 and 200 permutations are listed as inconclusive."

The criteria A1–A8 and E1–E5 were fixed in the pre-run audit, before any real-data run, and the results review could not move them [7]. The FDR-total windows are the half-width max(0.25·R, 5), integers inclusive, on R-12 [3, 7].

## 2. What was built

NEW (paths on `phase/P13-phase-pdc-permutations`) [6, 11]:
