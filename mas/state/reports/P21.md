## 1. What this phase reproduces

Origin, verbatim from the roadmap as quoted in `00_assignment.md`: "Professor (Oct 13): subject generalization, 'con todos menos con uno'; run on Google Colab (new user, credits provided)" [1]. The phase asks how a classifier trained on the other subjects labels a held-out subject at 1 s windows (stride 0.25 s), for four methods: the calibration-free FBCCA floor (`fbcca`), the in-fold CSP + FBCCA LDA (`lda`), the same LDA with each subject's own unlabeled whole-session normalization (`lda_subjnorm`, labelled unsupervised per-subject normalization (test-time, whole-session statistics = offline upper bound)), and three networks (`ccnn`, `compact_eegnet`, `eegnet`) [1, 2]. The pre-committed directions are R-16: LOSO below within-subject for trained methods, `fbcca` LOSO equal to within, and `lda_subjnorm` at least `lda` [2, 21]. The cohort decision is B003, option B: S01–S05 through P20, and every discovered subject for P21–P23, each reported as its own data source [19, 20]. The original project's LOSO cohort had 39 subjects without S020 [20].
