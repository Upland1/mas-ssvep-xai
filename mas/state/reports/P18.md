## 1. What this phase tests

Roadmap P18 [1]: replace edge-wise FDR as the only summary of connectivity by a graph-based statistic, and compare the sizes of the significant graphs. The built code keeps FDR beside NBS and saves both (settled point 3 [2]). Deviation 1 in §8 records the difference from the assignment title.

Tasks [1]: (1) `src/stats/nbs.py` (pseudo-z, components, `nbs_test`, `nbs_sweep`); (2) cohort script with `--stats {nbs,fdr}` (default `nbs`), `--nbs-thresholds`, `--nbs-primary 2.0`, a graph-size table and a threshold-sweep table; (3) two-tailed NBS at |z| > 2 in the between-frequency tests, and `--summary-only`. Acceptance, verbatim [1]: "NBS unit tests; cohort tables with both FDR and NBS; the report compares graph sizes across subjects and frequencies (record)."

Questions:
1. Does the NBS criterion (largest supra-threshold component; null = largest component of each trial-level permutation) agree with edge-wise FDR, cell by cell?
2. How large are the significant graphs across subjects, frequencies and methods, in % of possible edges?
3. Do the criteria flip between 200 and 1000 permutations (R7), and how do the results depend on the threshold?
4. Does the pairwise NBS (two-tailed |z| > 2 on the exchange null centred per edge) agree with the pairwise p values?

Pre-committed before the run [7]: checks V0–V8 (copied into `02_plan.md` [2]); reporting rules F1 (A-FDR flips), F2 (A-NBS flips), R1 (2 × 2 FDR × NBS), R2 (sizes in %), R3 (pairwise wording), R4 (pairwise agreement), R5 (borderline cells); predictions P1–P8. ## 6. Interpretation (the reviewer's reading unless stated)

NBS gains on extended moderate effects (4 FDR−/NBS+ cells) and loses on focal 1–2-edge results (9 FDR+/NBS− cells) [7, 9]. The 200-permutation comparison is not a fair test of sensitivity, because FDR is resolution-limited there (C-6).
- **One-tailed cohort NBS (S8).** The cohort NBS is upper-tailed only (stimulus > baseline). Decreases (for example alpha suppression at 10.91 and 8.57 Hz) are not tested (N3, C-4). Small graphs at those frequencies are therefore not evidence of no coupling change [7].

- **PDC.** Symmetrized PDC graphs are smaller than coherence and wPLI on average, but not smaller than PLV (D-4). Symmetrization cancels opposite-direction effects (§4.6). PDC NBS is therefore a test of undirected coupling change, not of directed flow [7].

A significant pairwise component is the excess beyond the band-level part of the exchange null, so NBS and `p_mag` can disagree for that reason alone (C1 [7]).
- **Leakage and fitting.** Not applicable: there is no classifier, the statistic uses only the permutation null, and the responder split uses classifier accuracy only (PF-21) [7].
