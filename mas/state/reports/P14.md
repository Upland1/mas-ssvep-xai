## 1. What this phase reproduces

Roadmap P14 origin: "Professor's follow-up: how to compare connectivity across stimulation frequencies; then whether the deltas are just SSVEP power or alpha blocking" [1]. Two questions, answered with the numbers below:
- **Q1.** How much of each method's delta is explained by target SNR and alpha change?
- **Q2.** Do 10.91 and 8.57 Hz behave differently from the other frequencies?

Acceptance, verbatim from the roadmap [1]: "CSVs and figures per §6; the report states, with numbers, how much of the delta is explained by target SNR and alpha change and whether 10.91/8.57 Hz behave differently (no fixed numeric target; compare qualitatively with R-13)."

Integrity (A0–A2), the qualitative comparisons (E1–E5) and the two questions were pre-committed in the pre-run audit, before any real-data run [7]. The P14 pairwise null is an exchangeability test, not a Δa = Δb test (P14 D-2 settled wording, decisions.md row 124) [5, 7].

**Reading (binding wording).**
- "Explained" means explained statistically across 25 non-independent cells, with SNR measured on the same windows [7].

- PLV and PDC: no reference values; the same wording rules apply [7].
- Alpha adds little everywhere (alpha_add ≤ 0.059), and the largest alpha-only centred value is PDC's 0.1274, which overlaps SNR [7, 10].
