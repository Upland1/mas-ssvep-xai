## 1. What this phase reproduces

First data-bearing step of the original project: `run_preprocessing.py` + `psd_extraction.py` (roadmap P02, "Origin") [1]. The continuous recording is band-passed once (1–60 Hz, order 4), cut into condition windows (201 fixation, 202 cue lookback, 101–105 stimulus), passed through a raw-stage Vpp/std gate, and stored as the arrays every Era-A/B script consumes: `X_time_windows (n, 7, 256)`, `X_psd_features (n, 217)`, `y_labels`. The question: does a rebuild written from `01_DATA_SPEC.md` give the window counts of R-1, and do the PSD-by-condition figures show the stimulation frequencies (S03–S05)? No classifier and no accuracy are involved.

### 4.6 The 201/202 twin finding (PF-13 on real data)

From the saved arrays (reviewer, `07_review.md` results review): for every 202 row, an identical row among the 201 rows of the same subject, else one shifted by 1–15 samples [8].

| Subject | Exact twin | Shifted twin | No twin |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | 40 | 0 | 0 |
| S02 | 35 | 5 | 0 |
| S03 | 36 | 3 | 0 |
| S04 | 33 | 7 | 0 |
| S05 | 34 | 6 | 0 |
| Total | 178 | 21 | 0 |

Check: `.venv/bin/python -c "print(178+21)"` → `199`. So 178 of the 199 saved 202 windows are bit-identical to a 201 second sub-window, and the other 21 are shifted by 8 samples = 0.03125 s (inside the §3 jitter of ≤ 0.04 s), sharing 248 of 256 samples. Consequence (decisions.md): the 202 "cue" window is the second before the 202 onset, which is the second 201 sub-window of its trial (PF-13, by design). 201 and 202 must never be used together as independent samples: not as two classes, not as a pooled no-stimulus set, and a 202 window must be grouped with its 201 twin in any trial-grouped CV (legacy trial ids would otherwise place them in different groups and leak). Baselines and the P17 rest class use 201 only. Stimulus windows are unaffected. The twin rejections also behave as predicted: S03 `201 sub 1 start 1536` and `202 start 1536` are rejected together [8].

## 9. Deviations, decisions, open questions

**fs statement (`02_REQUIREMENTS.md` §5.4).** P02 uses the header fs, 256 Hz, for the windows (`int(1.0 * fs)` = 256 samples) and the periodogram: 217 = 7 × 31 bins needs it (a 256-sample periodogram gives 31 bins in 5–35 Hz at fs 256 and 30 at fs 250, `.venv/bin/python` check in `07_review.md`) [8]. PF-04's hard-coded `fs = 250` is therefore not reproduced in P02; it enters with the P04/P05 extractors. Consequence: the legacy `X_psd_features` columns are on the true frequency axis; only extractors that recompute from `X_time_windows` with `fs=250` are affected. `team.yaml` P02 flags were corrected to `[]` accordingly (decisions.md 2026-10-24) [3].

- PO7 excluded: config v1 keeps 7 channels (`PO3, POz, PO4, PO8, O1, Oz, O2`), labelled `# PO7 hard-excluded (legacy)` (PF-05); channels are selected by name (PF-06). Reviewer: LEGACY (by design) [8].
