## 1. What this phase tests

Roadmap P17 [1]: add the fixation cross (201) as a sixth "rest" class, and add narrow harmonic (k·f, k = 1..3) and intermediate (midpoints of neighbouring targets) bands to the filter bank. Questions:

- **Bank effect (5 classes).** Does the narrow bank `harm` (base + harmonic bands) or `harm+mid` (+ intermediate bands) change 5-class accuracy against the five FBCSP bands `base`? (`--bank`)
- **Rest class (6 classes).** Can the fixation windows (201 only, uniform LDA priors) be separated from the five stimuli, and at what cost to stimulus accuracy? (`--classes 6`)
- **Build.** Do the new banks leave every P16 array byte-identical, and what does the longer guard change? [1, 2]

Acceptance, verbatim [1]: "all combinations produce CSVs; the report compares banks and 5 vs 6 classes with SDs over repeats (no preserved reference — record)."

Pre-committed rules, fixed by the reviewer before any number of the run existed [7]:
- Gain acceptance G1–G4 (§4.7). G1: the leak-control tests pass; G2: D0 identity, D1 cross-trial gap ≥ 2.0 s, D2 non-guard files identical; G3: no subject with base 5-class 1.0 s below 90 % reaches harm ≥ 97 % or gains ≥ +20 pp; G4: harm 2.0 s StimCorrect(6) + Missed(6) ≥ Smoothed(5) − 3 pp.
- Comparison rule: Δ = m_a − m_b, σ = √(SD_a² + SD_b²), w = one window in pp. Material: |Δ| ≥ max(2σ, 2w). Tie: |Δ| < max(σ, w). Otherwise small. A cohort claim needs a material difference of the same sign in ≥ 3 of 5 subjects and none of the opposite sign. Subjects with both arms ≥ 99 % count as ties at ceiling [7].
- Seven a-priori directions P1–P7 (§4.6), S03 excluded as seen (the ml-engineer's smoke run). A refuted direction is reported as refuted and is not a FAIL [7].

## 7. Interpretation (direction of every change, R-16)

Reviewer's reading, recorded as the reviewer's [7]. The directions below are those the run gave against the a-priori list in §4.6.

- **The narrow bank gain (harm − base).** Material on S01 (+12.35 / +12.60 pp) and S02 (+13.69 / +11.53 pp), at 1.0 s and 2.0 s; small at ceiling on S04 (+0.62); small on S05 (+0.96 / +0.85). S03 (+5.62 / +5.56 pp, material) was seen before the check (the ml-engineer's smoke run), so it is counted by the rule but is not the independent confirmation. The independent confirmation is S01 and S02 [7].
- **What the gain can be attributed to.** `harm` = 14 narrow bands (±1 Hz) at k·f for k = 1, 2, 3, with 60 Hz skipped. It includes the five fundamentals (k = 1) as narrow bands. The data cannot say whether the gain comes from the fundamentals or from the k = 2, 3 multiples (C-d). The report does not write "harmonic bands improve" or "harmonics carry the gain". The correct wording is "the narrow bank at the stimulus frequencies and their second and third multiples" [7].
- **Mechanism and leakage.** No fitted object sees test rows (CSP and scaler in-fold; priors fixed at 1/6), splits are trial-grouped with the runtime guard, and the mask is shared by all six runs. The pre-committed leak signature (G3) did not fire: the weak subjects S01 and S02 rose to 85.50 and 86.74 % at 1.0 s, not to the ceiling. The gain is accepted as "consistent with an SNR gain of the ±1 Hz bands at k·f, fundamentals included", not as proven genuine. A real-data label-permutation control was not required by the outcome and was not run [4, 7].
- **Intermediate bands.** `harm+mid` ties `harm` on every subject and metric, at 5 and 6 classes. The midpoints add nothing measurable [7].
- **Six classes: stimulus accuracy costs.** StimCorrect(6) is below Smoothed(5) in 15 of 15 cells. The loss is about Missed on S03–S05 only [7].
- **Six classes: rest errors move.** With the narrow banks, FalseAct falls on S01 and S02 (base 22.00 → harm 7.12 %; 16.08 → 3.24 %) and Missed rises on S01 (14.25 → 20.00 %). The gain is in rest discrimination on the two weakest subjects, not in stimulus discrimination [8].
- **Uniform priors.** With priors 1/6 the rest class (80 of 280 windows on S01, 0.29 of the six-class windows) is weighted lower than its frequency. This pushes errors toward FalseAct (rest predicted as stimulus) and away from Missed. The base S01 result (FalseAct 22.00 > Missed 14.25) is consistent with this. No run with frequency priors was made, so the effect is not isolated [7].
- **Class pattern.** The rest column is the main error sink for 105 (8.57 Hz) and 104 (10.91 Hz) on every bank (§4.5). This is consistent with alpha-band fixation activity overlapping the two lowest targets. It is descriptive only [7].
- **S04 mask change.** The longer guard removes one rest (201) and one cue (202) window; S04 has 75 rest windows against 76 in P16. No stimulus window and no 5-class number changes [7, 8].
- **Statistics.** SDs over ten repeats are split variability on fixed data. No p-values, and nothing is called significant [7].
