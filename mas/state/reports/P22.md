## 1. What this phase reproduces

Origin, verbatim from the roadmap as quoted in `00_assignment.md`: "Branch `feature/window-size-online-detection` (Oct 15)" [1]. The phase asks four questions on the P21 LOSO harness at 1 s windows (stride 0.25 s): (a) how far a matched within-subject LDA (`within_lda`, on exactly the LOSO channels) sits above the LOSO `lda` [1, 2]; (b) whether the channel list can be given explicitly, with the subjects whose gate dropped a named channel printed and left out [1, 2]; (c) whether a saved fold can be reused only under the same configuration and code, with a refusal otherwise (PF-37) [1, 2]; and (d) how many unlabeled trials of a new subject are needed before its normalization statistics are useful (warm-up, PF-35, PF-36) [1, 2]. The pre-committed directions are R-16 (LOSO below within for trained methods; `lda_subjnorm` at least `lda`; warm-up between `none` and `full` as K grows) and the nine predictions PR-1 to PR-7 with the verifier items V1 to V11, all written in `02_plan.md` [2, 7].
