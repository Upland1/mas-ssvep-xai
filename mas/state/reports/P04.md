**Scope note (mandatory).** Every P04 accuracy is computed with the legacy window-level `StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)` (R1, PF-01). Windows of one trial sit in both the training and the test fold, so the CV is **leaky**. The "smoothed" numbers use the legacy 2-window causal average without trial reset (R8, PF-02); the arrays are ordered by condition, so the average crosses trial boundaries. The FBCCA features use fs 250 on 256 Hz data (PF-04) and sub-band filtering on each 1 s window (PF-14). Both CV leakage and the trial-boundary smoothing are fixed in P11; the FBCCA fs and filtering are fixed in P16. **None of the P04 numbers is comparable with the R-6 flagship (`mas/RULES.md`) or with any later phase as an equal.** R-6 values appear in this report only as a diagnostic reference.

## 1. What this phase reproduces

The original project's first model benchmark (roadmap P04, "Origin"): a 37-feature hybrid (32 harmonic-PSD features from POz, O1, Oz, O2 plus 5 FBCCA scores), six sklearn classifiers, a dual-branch fusion CNN of 10,005 parameters, per-subject exported models and LDA-weight heatmaps [1]. The question answered is what a first hybrid benchmark gives on the legacy arrays under the original's leaky CV and no-reset smoothing, and what the exported linear model weights look like. Chronology relative to P05 is approximate and the phase uses only legacy arrays [1, 2]. Acceptance (roadmap, verbatim in `00_assignment.md`): all scripts run; figures and CSV exist; `DualBranchFusionCNN` has 10,005 parameters (R-8); numbers recorded; the Reviewer notes the leaky CV and the trial-boundary smoothing [1, 2].

## 7. Interpretation

**Reviewer findings (results review, `07_review.md`) [10].** The scribe copied the numbers and checked the arithmetic (section 4 and the shown commands); the readings are the reviewer's.

1. **No leakage-driven inflation on S01 and S02 for the linear models.** Leaky LDA at 1.0 s is 50.00 % (S01) and 51.26 % (S02), 11.00 and 4.86 points below R-6. The P03 expectation (an S01/S02 accuracy far above R-6 would be a leakage suspect, `mas/state/reports/P03.md` §7) was not met. The size of the leak itself is **not measured here**; it is measured in P11 with grouped CV on the same features [10, 13].
2. **Train versus test gap (PF-30, as cited by the reviewer).** Fusion CNN mean training-fold accuracy versus pooled test accuracy: S01 95.38 vs 51.50 % (gap 43.88 points), S02 98.12 vs 53.77 % (44.35), S03 98.75 vs 89.00 % (9.75), S04 100.00 vs 99.49 % (0.51), S05 100.00 vs 100.00 % (0.00) [8]. S01/S02 are a generalization problem (high train, near-LDA test), not an optimization problem. The CNN beats LDA by 1.50 and 2.51 points on S01/S02 (`CNN 1.0 s − LDA 1.0 s`, section 4.4). The CNN gains on S03 (+6.00) and S05 (+4.50) are **inconclusive** until P11.
   Check (CSV values, `outputs/reports/batch_run_subjects.csv`): `.venv/bin/python -c "print(round(100*(0.95375-0.515),3), round(100*(0.9811713836477987-0.5376884422110553),3), round(100*(0.9875-0.89),3), round(100*(0.9949494949494949-1.0),3))"` → `43.875 44.348 9.75 -0.505` (S04 is test minus train, i.e. the gap 0.505 in absolute value). The reviewer rounds the gaps to 43.88, 44.35, 9.75 and 0.51 (`07_review.md`, results review, interp. 2) [8, 10].
3. **Smoothed gains are not 2 s decisions.** LDA smoothed − 1.0 s: S01 +5.50, S02 +12.56, S03 +12.00, S04 0.00, S05 +2.00 points. CNN: S01 +11.50, S02 +10.55, S03 +6.00, S04 −1.52, S05 −1.50 points [8]. The arrays are ordered by condition then time (`01_DATA_SPEC` §5.1, as cited by the reviewer), so the no-reset average crosses about 39 trial boundaries per subject (`.venv/bin/python -c "print(200/5-1)"` → `39.0`; 200 windows, 5 per trial), of which only 4 are class changes. Most of the averaging is with a same-class window of another trial. S04 and S05 CNN smoothing loses exactly 3 windows each (`(0.9797979797979798-0.9949494949494949)*198` → `-3.0`; `(0.985-1.0)*200` → `-3.0`). The large S02 and S03 gains cannot be split into within-trial and cross-trial parts here: **inconclusive**. P11 (reset per trial) gives the honest number.
4. **Classifier spread.** Within a subject the six classifiers differ by up to 10.50 points (S01: Shrinkage-LDA 50.00 vs Linear SVM 39.50; `.venv/bin/python -c "print(50.00-39.50)"` → `10.5`). Single CV run, no seed variation; no ranking claim. Shrinkage-LDA is best at 1.0 s only on S01.
5. **PF-04 expectation not supported (descriptive).** The pre-committed expectation was weaker FBCCA weights for 101 and 102 at fs 250 (`07_review.md` pre-run check 5). S04 full-data LDA FBCCA diagonal weights 101–105 are 3.63 / 6.45 / 6.84 / 4.97 / 4.51; 101 is the weakest but 102 is not. The fs-independent PSD weights also weakest for 101 (max 3.10 vs 4.71–6.01 for 102–104). The weak 101 term is at least partly the subject's own response. The quantified fs effect is for P16 [10, 13].
