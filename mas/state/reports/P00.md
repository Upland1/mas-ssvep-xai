## 7. Interpretation

- All 10 delivered recordings are at 256 Hz with the 11 expected channels and the full calibration pattern (40 onsets of 8 per target for 101–105, 40 each of 200–203); no hard check fired, including the trailing-bytes check [9, 7].
- The p99 definition (99th percentile of |x| pooled over PO7…O2 on the raw file) reproduces §4 on 10/10 files with max |Δ| 0.043 µV across values from 23.8 to 1013.8 µV; the reviewer judged it settled and no reference consultation was needed [7].
- Session start is the first TIMESTAMP sample truncated to the minute. All 10 strings equal §4, but truncation and rounding agree whenever the seconds are below 30, so rounding is not excluded by the data [7].
- Conclusive: the 10 delivered files agree with their §4 rows. Not conclusive: anything about the 13 absent sessions; the `online`, `joined` and `C`/`mC` code paths are validated on synthetic files only [5, 7]. P00 does not validate the PO7 exclusion, the legacy `fs=250` assumption or any window count (P01–P02).
