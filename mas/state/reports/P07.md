## 1. What this phase reproduces

Roadmap P07 origin: "add a criterion to automatically discard noisy channels"; the montage legend showed 8 channels while the pipeline used 7, with PO7 hard-excluded [1]. Question: can a two-tier gate (subject-level channel drop, then window rejection on the surviving channels) restore PO7 without changing the legacy 7-channel results, and does the PF-07 `vpp_min` regression reproduce on S03 [1, 2]?

## 7. Interpretation

- **Conclusive.** The P07 path on 8 channels, with tier 1 dropping PO7, rebuilds the S03 P02 arrays: labels equal and `X_time` maximum difference 0.0. The gate reproduces R-6 counts and channel columns for all five subjects. On the legacy arrays tier 1 drops no channel.
- **Conclusive about PF-07.** The count reproduces; the cause is PO7 `vpp_high`. With PO7 removed, `vpp_min` 5.0 and 0.5 give identical 317/3.
- **Not conclusive.** Accuracies are legacy and leaky (R1, R8, fs 250, R5); they carry no method claim and no R-6 comparison. The 8-channel path was run for S03 only; S01, S02, S04 and S05 were not preprocessed with PO7. Feature width now depends on the tier-1 result per subject (`harmonic 8 × surviving cluster`, PSD `C' × F`), so per-subject feature sets must not be pooled (LOSO) before P16 (`07_review.md` note 4).
- **Leakage.** The gate uses fixed limits and no labels; tier 1 is a per-subject decision on unlabelled windows, which R2 permits. CSP, scaler and LDA are fitted in-fold (`07_review.md`, R2). P07 computes no statistics.
