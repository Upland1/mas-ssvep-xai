## 1. What this phase reproduces

Origin, verbatim from the roadmap as quoted in `00_assignment.md`: "Branch `test/GNN-testing-LOSO` (Oct 21): the edge-gated connectivity GNN trained on all subjects but one, with a within-subject reference on the same windows" [1]. The phase trains the P15 edge-gated connectivity GNN under leave-one-subject-out on the Era C built folders, with a within-subject reference on exactly the same windows and channels, and places it next to the `benchmark_loso.py` rows (fbcca, lda, lda_subjnorm, networks). It asks whether the GNN generalizes across subjects, whether the coherence edges add anything (R-15 direction), whether subject-level normalization helps, and how to read train-high, test-low folds under PF-30 [1, 2]. The directions and nine predictions (PR-1 to PR-9) and the verifier items (V1 to V12) were written in `02_plan.md` after the builder previews, and are plausibility checks, not blind predictions [2, 10].

**Fold configuration (PF-37).** A fold configuration stores the channels, the training subjects and their window counts, the built-folder `git_commit` of each subject, the `config_hash` of `configs/pipeline_config.yaml`, window, stride, `fs`, classes, seeds, hyperparameters including `threads` and `device`, `git_head` and `git_dirty` recorded once at process start [3, 5]. `check_fold` refuses any difference except `data_root` and the head and dirty state, with "use a new --out-dir" [3, 5]. **Fold-count and within rules.** A within fold trains on the subject's own windows; the within `Folds` column is 1 per subject, which means one within protocol run per subject with five CV folds in `folds.csv` (50 rows = 2 methods × 5 subjects × 5 folds) [12, 13, own].
