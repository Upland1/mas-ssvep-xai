## 1. What this phase reproduces

Roadmap P10 derives from two items: "implement simple EEG data augmentation" and "networks + augmentation". The first A/B showed EEGNet near chance on S01 and S02, which prompted a train-accuracy diagnostic [1]. Two questions under the legacy protocol: (i) does training-fold augmentation raise the EEGNet cohort accuracy by an amount in the range recorded for the original leaky runs (R-9a) [1, 3]; (ii) do S01 and S02 show near-chance test accuracy with high un-augmented training accuracy, which PF-30 reads as a generalization failure rather than an optimization failure [4]. Compact-CNN and ShallowConvNet run the same A/B as diagnostics only.

**Augmentation definitions (as coded, `augmentation.py`) [10].**
- Gaussian noise: `w + N(0, (std_frac · σ_c)²)` per channel c, σ_c the standard deviation over the window's own time axis; `std_frac` = 0.05 (`:27–31`).
- Scale: one factor k ~ U(0.9, 1.1) per window, applied to all channels (`:34–37`).
- Circular shift: integer s ∈ [−⌊0.1·T⌋, ⌊0.1·T⌋] applied with `np.roll` along time (`:40–44`); T is the window length (256 samples for the R-8 shape [3]).
- Mixup: partner drawn uniformly from the anchor's class (`:80–82`; the partner may be the anchor itself, N1); λ ~ Beta(0.4, 0.4), folded to `max(λ, 1 − λ)` (`:47–53`).
- Order and probability: mixup → scale → shift → noise, each applied with p = 0.5 (`:20`, `:59–62`, `:77–88`), from an own `numpy` Generator seeded `42 + fold` (`eegnet_cv.py:113`; `augmentation.py:69`). The torch RNG is not used (PF-28).
- Scope: training fold only, after per-window z-scoring (R2) [10].

## 7. Interpretation

**Acceptance clause 1 (R-9a).** Verbatim from `00_assignment.md`: "EEGNet cohort augmentation gain in the range recorded by R-9a (first leaky run +3.00 pp 1.0 s / +1.79 pp 2.0 s with 4/5 subjects improved and S02 regressed; the last leaky run before the fix +7.34 pp 1.0 s) — explain where yours falls" [1, 3]. **Met.**
- *Position.* The 1.0 s cohort gain of +3.11 pp is inside [+3.00, +7.34] pp, 0.11 pp above the lower edge and 4.23 pp below the upper edge (`python3 -c "print(round(3.11-3.00,2), round(7.34-3.11,2))"` → `0.11 4.23`). It is nearer the first leaky run than the last. The position is not resolved more finely: one CPU run, one seed set. The P08 CPU–CUDA gap of 9 windows corresponds to 2.04 pp on a five-subject mean (`python3 -c "print(round(100*9/197/5**0.5,2))"` → `2.04`), so the margin is within run-to-run scale [5]. Nothing here shows that this setup matches the original's first or last code version.
- *Per subject, in windows (1.0 s).* S01 +15 (of 200), S02 −3 (196), S03 +10 (197), S04 +2 (197), S05 +7 (200) [7]. Only S01 and S03 exceed the nine-window noise line (the P08 device gap, decision row 83 [5]). S02 is within noise.
- *Cohort without S01.* The mean of the other four subjects is +2.02 pp (`python3 -c "print(round((-1.53+5.08+1.01+3.5)/4,2))"` → `2.02`), so the cohort gain rests mainly on S01 and S03. Within S01, four of five folds improved (per-fold test Δ +12.5, +5.0, −2.5, +10.0, +12.5 pp, from the logs [8]).
- *Direction.* Four of five subjects positive: one-sided probability under a fair-coin null is 6/32 = 0.1875 (`python3 -c "print((5+1)/32)"` → `0.1875`) [own]. This is not a significance claim: folds and windows are not independent under window-level CV.
- *2.0 s.* +2.90 pp against +1.79 pp is inside ±3 pp (Δ +1.11) [3].
- *Regularisation signature.* ON lowers the EEGNet train accuracy on the un-augmented fold (S01 83.25 → 73.88, S02 81.38 → 70.42, cohort 92.37 → 88.02 %; differences 9.37, 10.96, 4.35 pp) while test accuracy rises on S01 and S03. This is the expected behaviour of augmentation as a regulariser, and the training pass is not augmented (`eegnet_cv.py:117`) [4, 10].

**Acceptance clause 2 (PF-30).** Verbatim: "S01/S02 near chance with high training accuracy is reproduced and interpreted (generalization failure, not optimization failure)" [1]. **Met.**
- OFF test accuracy: S01 28.00 % (56/200), S02 27.04 % (53/196), both ≤ 40 % (twice chance) [7]. OFF train accuracy on the un-augmented fold: 83.25 % and 81.38 %, both ≥ 70 % [7]. The OFF-train < 40 % warning did not fire [8].
- Reading: EEGNet fits most of its training fold, so it does not fail to optimise; it fails to transfer to held-out windows. That is a generalization failure, as PF-30 specifies [4]. The leaky split makes transfer easier (sibling windows of test trials sit in the training fold), and EEGNet still stays near chance.
- The same S01/S02 difficulty appears in the other networks: OFF test S01 / S02 is 58.00 / 37.24 % for ShallowConvNet and 24.00 / 32.65 % for Compact-CNN, each with 100.00 % train [7].
- ON arm: S01 35.50 % (train 73.88), S02 25.51 % (train 70.42). Augmentation does not change the diagnosis.

**Compact-CNN (diagnostic; no verdict).** Cohort Δ +14.12 / +11.39 pp; train 100 % in both arms, so OFF is overfitting and the ON gain is regularisation of that overfit [7].
- S04: 53.30 → 96.45 % (+43.15 pp, +85 windows). S04 is ≥ 98.48 % for EEGNet and ShallowConvNet, so the Compact-CNN OFF level is the anomaly [7].
- S02: −10.71 / −14.28 pp (−21 / −28 windows); ON 21.94 / 20.92 %, at chance. Beyond the noise line, same sign as R-9b's S02 (−7.14) [3, 7]. Reported, not explained; one seed set.
- **Coincidence, not a reproduction.** Compact-CNN S04 Δ = 85/197 = 43.15 pp equals R-9b S04 Δ = 97.97 − 54.82 = 43.15 pp (`python3 -c "print(round(100*85/197,2), round(97.97-54.82,2))"` → `43.15 43.15`), but the OFF and ON levels differ (53.30 / 96.45 vs 54.82 / 97.97). Different CV, different quantity [3, 7].
- Compact S03 2.0 s OFF 62.94 equals the P09 value. This is the same run (regression row), not a coincidence [5, 9].
- EEGNet S02 1.0 s OFF 27.04 % (53/196) has the same percentage as R-9b S02 OFF 27.04 %. Not interpreted; no reproduction claimed [3, 7].
- Kernel: Compact-CNN is still the fs 250 network, K = 29 (PF-04; PF-31 naming) [4].

**ShallowConvNet (diagnostic; no verdict).** Cohort Δ +2.01 / +2.72 pp. Every per-subject 1.0 s Δ is at most 10 windows (S01 +10, S03 +8, S05 +2, S02 0, S04 0), so no subject shows a detectable effect [7]. Train 100 % in both arms.

**Leakage audit of fitted objects.** No fitted object beyond the networks. Per-window z-scoring uses no other window. The augmentation generator is per fold and draws only from the training fold. No validation split, no early stopping, fixed 150 epochs. No hyperparameter search: p, α, std_frac, scale range and shift fraction are the roadmap values [1, 10].

**Legacy caveats.**
1. All A/B numbers use window-level CV (R1, PF-01) [2, 4]. Mixup, shift and scale can let a network memorise trial-specific structure shared between sibling windows across the split. The leaky gain may therefore be inflated or masked. The direction cannot be known before P11.
2. The 2.0 s column is the legacy `i % 5` average over the clean array (R8, PF-02) [2, 4]. Clean counts are not multiples of five (S02 196, S03 197, S04 197), so the 5-blocks are misaligned. It is not a 2 s decision accuracy.
3. P11 measures the leak-free A/B: `StratifiedGroupKFold` on trial ids and trial-aware smoothing [2, 3]. No P10 delta may be quoted as an augmentation effect without the "leaky" label.
4. Statistics: the unit is the window. There is no permutation or paired test and no multiple-comparison correction across 3 models × 5 subjects × 2 horizons. Only the R-9a band is an acceptance criterion; everything else is descriptive [2].
5. CPU only, one seed set. The P08 CPU–CUDA gap of 9 windows shows device effects at this scale [5]; not measured here.
6. The per-script P07 gate (R5 legacy) and the fs 250 label (K = 29) continue to P16 under the era matrix [2].

**Era-A close.** P10 is the last phase under the legacy window-level CV (R1) and `i % 5` smoothing (R8); both are enforced from P11 [2]. All P04–P10 network numbers, including these CSVs, are frozen under `reports/legacy_leaky/` and superseded by P11 [7].
