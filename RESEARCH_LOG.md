# Research log

The research history of the rebuild as one chronological narrative, P00 → P23. Each stage
states its aim, what was built, the numbers it produced (with the CSV or log they come
from), each problem found, each fix with a **Before → after** line, and the open items it
handed on. Phase reports are the full record (`mas/state/reports/Pxx.md`); the numbers here
are copied from the CSVs under `reports/` (git-ignored; regenerated by `make all`, see
README). Legacy numbers are labelled `legacy` and are not comparable with the corrected ones.
Reference numbers of the original project are labelled R-x and are targets for diagnosis,
never results of this repository (`mas/04_REFERENCE_RESULTS.md`).

Eras: **A** (P00–P10) legacy pipeline reproduced as it was, leaky window-level CV;
**B** (P11–P15) trial-leakage fix, connectivity and the first GNN; **C** (P16–P23) continuous
filtering at the recorded 256 Hz, detection, pseudo-online and leave-one-subject-out (LOSO).
